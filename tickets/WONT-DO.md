# Won't Do — explicitly declined

These requests came up in the field review but were rejected by OP's core-values stewards. Recording them so they don't get re-litigated each cycle.

### Soften the overall into a weighted-average / best-category score
Weakest-link is a core value and statistically meaningful (overall = lowest category). Averaging rewards specialists and destroys the well-roundedness signal OP exists to give. The retention concern is met by RM-004 (radar-primary view), not by changing the math.

### Body-composition / aesthetics axis (body-fat %, waist-to-height, lean mass)
Out of scope for a functional-performance standard. Invites unvalidated, body-image-laden metrics. Driven by physique/boutique business models, not the populations OP serves. Track separately if desired.

### Operator-configurable / brand-safe tier relabeling (hiding 'Beginner')
Conflicts with the OPEN shared, honest vocabulary that makes an 'OP level' portable, and with the trademark that exists to prevent brand-specific reskins presented as OP. Operators may relabel in private forks; it will not be a sanctioned OP feature.

### American kettlebell swing as an accepted variant
Settled, documented project rule: Russian swings only. A second swing form reduces comparability for no accuracy gain. Gym-agnostic means not brand-locked, not 'accepts every movement variant a single market prefers'.

### Banded / assisted 'partial-credit' thresholds inside benchmark scoring
No citable dataset for 'a banded pull-up equals X'. Fabricating thresholds violates the no-unsourced-numbers rule. The on-ramp need belongs in progressions.md / Foundation Milestones as coaching guidance, not scored benchmarks.

### Re-anchor or re-source levels 1-7 to a competition dataset
OP's percentile model is built on the broad trained population; re-sourcing to competition data would mis-serve the ~95% OP exists for. The defensible answer is the ADDITIVE competitor tier (RM-015) plus the 'elite' relabel (RM-005).

### Imperial units as a first-class or default path
Metric-native, clean-5kg-multiple authoring is a deliberate gym-agnostic choice and a project policy. A render-only display toggle (data stays metric) is at most a low-priority community contribution and a maintainer policy call — never a default that re-centers the standard on one market.
